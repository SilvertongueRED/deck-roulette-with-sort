## DeckRoulette with Sort — 1.3.27-sort.4

### Changed
- **Recently Opened now also counts picking a list with the roulette button.** A collection counts as "opened" when you pick it in DeckRoulette, visit it in your Steam Library, or play a game from it, whichever happened last. Picking a list now moves it to the top of Recently Opened straight away.
- **Recently Rouletted is unchanged**: it still only tracks roulette picks.

### Added
- The diagnostics readout (Customize Shortcuts → Shortcuts) now also shows how many roulette picks are recorded.

### Notes
- Library visits are still recorded when Steam reports a collection page. The previous diagnostics showed Steam reporting page changes correctly, but no collection page had been visited yet.
