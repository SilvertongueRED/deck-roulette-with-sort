## DeckRoulette with Sort — 1.3.27-sort.2

### Changed
- **Sort button labels are reversed.** The button now shows the mode you will switch **to** (`🔀 Sort: Recently Opened`), and the line under it shows the mode you are **currently using** (`Current: Recently Rouletted`).
- **Recently Opened now counts opening a collection.** Going into a collection in your Steam Library puts it at the top of Recently Opened, as does playing a game from it. Whichever happened last wins.

### Notes
- Collection visits are recorded from the moment you install this version. Collections you opened earlier fall back to their last-played order until you open them again.
- Opening a list with the roulette button still counts as **Recently Rouletted**, not Recently Opened.
